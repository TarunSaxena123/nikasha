Phase 1: Chart Layout Setup
 1. Timeframe: Set the NIFTY 50 Spot Index chart strictly to the 15-Minute interval.
 2. Central Pivot Range (CPR): Search for 'Pivot Points Standard' in the Indicators (Fx) menu.
  Change the Type to Traditional and Timeframe to Daily in settings.
  In Style settings, check only P, TC, BC (the 3 CPR lines), R1 (Call target), and S1 (Put target).
 3. Momentum Guard Line: Add 'Moving Average Exponential' (EMA) and change its length to 20.
 4. Opening Range: At 09:30 AM, draw 2 horizontal lines marking the absolute High and Low of the first 15-min
 candle.
Phase 2: Rules of Engagement (Exact Entry Filters)
■ Call Option (CE) Buying Rules:
  Core Bias: The first 15-min candle must open and trade completely above the daily CPR lines.
  Trigger: A subsequent 15-min candle must close its body completely above the Opening Range High line.
  Strike Selection: Buy the weekly contract exactly 100 points In-The-Money (ITM) below Nifty spot via Market
 Order.
■ Put Option (PE) Buying Rules:
  Core Bias: The first 15-min candle must open and trade completely below the daily CPR lines.
  Trigger: A subsequent 15-min candle must close its body completely below the Opening Range Low line.
  Strike Selection: Buy the weekly contract exactly 100 points In-The-Money (ITM) above Nifty spot via Market
 Order.

 1. Time Window Cut-off: Only enter triggers occurring before 11:30 AM. Ignore afternoon breakouts entirely to
 bypass low volume and high Theta decay traps.
2. Wide CPR Filter: If the daily printed TC and BC lines have a wide horizontal gap, skip trading. Wide CPR
 mathematically indicates a sideways trend.
3. Over-Sized Candle Filter: If the breakout candle moves Nifty more than 70 points in a single 15-minute frame, it
 is overextended. Skip the entry.
4. Daily Limit Boundary: Strictly 1 trade per day. Win or lose, turn off your trading application as soon as the
